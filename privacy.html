Privacy Policy

HVAC Lead Recovery · Last updated September 15, 2026

HVAC Lead Recovery ("we", "us") is operated by Jason Dominic, a sole proprietor. This policy explains what information we collect through our SMS messaging program, why we collect it, and how it is handled.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third parties.

Information we collect

When you call our published business phone number and the call is not answered, we send you a text message so we can help with your request. Through that phone call and the text conversation that follows, we collect:

  • Your mobile phone number (captured from your inbound call)
  • Your name
  • The HVAC problem or service you need
  • The service address or ZIP code where work is needed
  • Your preferred appointment time
  • The contents and timestamps of the text messages you exchange with us

We do not collect payment card details, Social Security numbers, government identifiers, or health information through this messaging program. Please do not send that information by text.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to the service request you started by calling us — to understand the problem, to pass the request to the servicing contractor, and to arrange a visit. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Who we share it with

Your service request is provided to the HVAC contractor who will perform the work, because that is the purpose of the request.

We also rely on two service providers to operate the messaging program:

  • Twilio — delivers and receives the text messages.
  • OpenAI — generates the text of our replies from the conversation.

These providers process the information solely to provide those services to us. They are not permitted to use it for their own marketing. Beyond this, we share information only where required by law.

How long we keep it

We retain service requests and message history for as long as needed to serve the request and keep ordinary business records, and delete them when no longer needed. You may ask us to delete your information using the contact details below.

How we protect it

Information is stored on access-controlled systems and transmitted over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to what is needed to operate the service.

Your choices

You can stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP to any text from us. Your opt-out is honored automatically and you will receive no further messages unless you contact us again. Reply HELP for assistance.

You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information we hold about you by contacting us.

Children

This service is intended for adults arranging HVAC service. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
