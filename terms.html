Program name

HVAC Lead Recovery, operated by Jason Dominic, a sole proprietor.

Program description

When you call our published business phone number and we are unable to answer, we send you a text message so we can still help you. Through that conversation we collect the details of your service request — the problem, your name, the service address, and your preferred appointment time — and pass it to the servicing contractor, who then contacts you. Messages are conversational and are sent only to the number that called us.

How to opt out: reply STOP to any message from us at any time. You will receive a single confirmation and no further messages.

How to get help: reply HELP to any message, or use the contact details below.

How you consent

You consent to receive text messages from us by placing a phone call to our business number. We reply by text only to the number that called us, and only about the service request that call started. We do not purchase, rent, or upload phone number lists, and we never message a number that has not called us first.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. A typical service request involves about four to six messages while we gather the details you provide. We do not send recurring marketing messages.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge for the messages, but your mobile carrier's standard rates apply to messages you send and receive. Check your plan with your carrier.

Supported carriers

This program is available on major U.S. wireless carriers, including AT&T, Verizon Wireless, T-Mobile, Sprint, U.S. Cellular, Boost, MetroPCS, Virgin Mobile, and Cricket. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Delivery

Message delivery depends on your carrier's network and is not guaranteed. If your request is time-sensitive or involves a safety concern, call us directly rather than relying on text.

Safety

We do not diagnose HVAC problems by text and we do not quote prices by text. If you smell gas, see smoke, or suspect carbon monoxide, leave the building immediately and call 911 and your gas utility. Do not wait for a text reply.

Appointments

A preferred time collected by text is a request, not a confirmed appointment. The servicing contractor confirms scheduling directly with you.

Privacy

Information collected through this program is handled as described in our Privacy Policy. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
